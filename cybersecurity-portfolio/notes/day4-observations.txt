
Day 4 - Product Reviews API Observation

Target:
OWASP Juice Shop running locally at http://127.0.0.1:3000

Request:
GET /rest/products/1/reviews

Observed result:
HTTP 200 OK in Burp Repeater.
The response contained JSON review data.

Fields examined:
Review message, author, product association, and likes count.

Interpretation:
The endpoint returned review information associated with
product ID 1. The product field identifies the product
associated with a review.

Security assessment:
Public product reviews may be expected application behavior.
This observation alone does not establish an IDOR vulnerability.

Limitations:
Only the observed request and response are documented.
No vulnerability has been confirmed from this observation.
